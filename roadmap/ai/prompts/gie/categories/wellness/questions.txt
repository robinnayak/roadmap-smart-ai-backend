Wellness focus areas:
- Current wellness baseline
- Primary challenge
- Daily time available
- Existing practices
- Trigger context
- Motivation driver

Example question angles:
- What does your stress, sleep, or emotional baseline look like right now?
- What challenge feels most urgent to improve first?
- How much time can you realistically give this each day?
- What practices have you already tried, if any?
- When or where does this challenge usually show up?
- Why does improving this matter to you right now?
