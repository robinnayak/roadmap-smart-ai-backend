Fitness focus areas:
- Current fitness baseline
- Previous training experience
- Weekly training availability
- Session length capacity
- Injury or recovery constraints
- Motivation driver

Example question angles:
- What does your current fitness or training baseline look like?
- What experience do you already have with this kind of training?
- How many days each week can you realistically train?
- How long can a typical session be?
- Are there any injuries, recovery limits, or access constraints?
- Why is this goal important to you now?
