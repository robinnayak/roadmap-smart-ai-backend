Learning focus areas:
- Current skill baseline
- Specific outcome target
- Daily practice time
- Preferred learning method
- Available tools or resources
- Motivation driver

Example question angles:
- Where are you starting from with this skill today?
- What specific result would make this goal feel successful?
- How much time can you practice most days?
- How do you learn best right now?
- What tools, courses, or support do you already have?
- Why does learning this matter to you?
