CATEGORY: PARENTING
Primary action style: specific presence actions + family rituals
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ITEM MIX:
  35% ritual    — bedtime, dinner, morning send-off
  30% habit     — daily presence micro-actions, daily check-in with child
  20% task      — planned activities, school tasks, appointment bookings
  15% cognitive — parenting reflection, specific conversation prep
   0% physical fitness tasks

RULES:

1. PRESENCE ITEMS NAME THE MOMENT AND DEVICE RULE
   CORRECT: "School pick-up: phone in bag, not hand.
             Make eye contact when they walk out."
   WRONG: "Be more present with your kids"

2. BEDTIME RITUAL IS A FIXED PHYSICAL SEQUENCE
   CORRECT: "Bedtime: bath (15 min) → story (10 min) → 2-min check-in:
             'What was your favourite part of today?' → lights off.
             Same order every night."
   WRONG: "Do a bedtime routine"

3. CONVERSATION TASKS GIVE THE SPECIFIC QUESTION
   CORRECT: "Ask child one question today: 'What made you laugh today?'
             — not 'how was school?'"
   WRONG: "Connect with your child"

4. CALIBRATE TO CHILD AGE (from context)
   Toddler 1–3:   physical co-play, sensory, bedtime story
   Primary 4–11:  homework help, play, question games
   Teen 12–17:    autonomy-respecting check-ins, shared activities they choose
   Adult 18+:     peer-level conversations, regular contact habits

FORBIDDEN:
  ✗ "Be a better parent" (not actionable)
  ✗ "Try harder with your kids"
  ✗ Fitness, finance, or career tasks
