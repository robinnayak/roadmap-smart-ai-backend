CATEGORY: WELLNESS
Primary action style: body-based rituals + regulated self-monitoring
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ITEM MIX:
  35% ritual    — transitional anchors morning/sleep/pre-trigger
  30% habit     — daily check-ins, mood logs, coping practices
  20% physical  — gentle movement only (walks, breathing)
  15% cognitive — journaling with specific prompts, therapy prep
   0% high-intensity physical items
   0% financial or career tasks

RULES:

1. BREATHING TASKS GIVE THE EXACT TECHNIQUE
   CORRECT: "Sit upright. Inhale 4 counts. Hold 4. Exhale 6. Repeat 6 times."
   WRONG: "Do some deep breathing"

2. JOURNALING TASKS INCLUDE THE SPECIFIC PROMPT
   CORRECT: "Open journal. Write 5 min to: 'What am I carrying right now
             that I could put down?'"
   WRONG: "Journal about your day"

3. SLEEP TASKS ARE PHYSICAL ENVIRONMENT ACTIONS
   CORRECT: "Put phone on charge in kitchen — not bedroom. Do this at 9:30 PM."
   WRONG: "Try to sleep earlier"
   WRONG: "Avoid screens before bed"

4. MOOD TRACKING IS A SPECIFIC LOG
   CORRECT: "Open DayOneGoal. Rate today: energy (1–5), mood (1–5),
             anxiety (1–5). Add one sentence of context."
   WRONG: "Check in with how you are feeling"

5. THERAPY DAYS ARE EVENT-TRIGGERED
   If therapy_active and today is therapy_day:
   Generate pre-therapy journal prompt + post-therapy integration note.

FORBIDDEN:
  ✗ High-intensity exercise tasks
  ✗ "Fix yourself" framing — frame as adding, not fixing
  ✗ Financial or productivity tasks
  ✗ Any task framed as improving mood (reframe as physical action)
