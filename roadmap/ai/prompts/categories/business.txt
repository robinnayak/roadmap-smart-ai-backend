CATEGORY: BUSINESS
Primary action style: ship + sell + talk to customers
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ITEM MIX:
  40% task      — ship, send, publish, deploy specific things
  25% cognitive — build sessions, content creation, strategy reviews
  20% habit     — daily outreach, daily metric check, daily content
  15% ritual    — work-start ritual, weekly review ritual
   0% physical

RULES:

1. SHIP TASKS NAME THE SPECIFIC THING BEING SHIPPED
   CORRECT: "Push today's commit: complete the user login flow"
   CORRECT: "Publish this week's newsletter — draft already written"
   WRONG: "Work on your product"
   WRONG: "Create content"

2. CUSTOMER TASKS NAME THE ACTION AND THE NUMBER
   CORRECT: "Send 3 cold outreach messages — use the template in /docs"
   CORRECT: "Book 1 customer discovery call — send Calendly link today"
   WRONG: "Talk to customers"

3. VALIDATION STAGE: NO BUILD TASKS
   If business_stage is 'idea' or 'validation':
   Generate ZERO build tasks. All tasks must be customer conversations
   or hypothesis tests until validation milestone is confirmed.
   CORRECT: "Talk to 1 potential customer — ask about their problem,
             not your solution"

4. METRIC REVIEW PRODUCES A SPECIFIC NUMBER
   CORRECT: "Open dashboard. Record: signups this week, revenue this week.
             Note one metric moving in wrong direction."
   WRONG: "Check your metrics"

FORBIDDEN:
  ✗ "Research your market" without a specific deliverable
  ✗ "Think about your business model"
  ✗ "Plan your strategy" as a standalone task
  ✗ Build tasks during validation phase
