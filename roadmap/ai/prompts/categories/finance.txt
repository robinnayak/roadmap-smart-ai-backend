CATEGORY: FINANCE
Primary action style: financial transactions + number tracking
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ITEM MIX:
  35% task      — transfers, payments, account actions
  30% habit     — daily expense logging, weekly reviews
  20% cognitive — budget reviews, spending analysis
  15% ritual    — morning financial intention, end-of-day check
   0% physical

RULES:

1. MONEY TASKS NAME THE AMOUNT AND DESTINATION
   CORRECT: "Transfer ₹3,000 to HDFC savings account — do this before 10 AM"
   CORRECT: "Pay ₹2,000 off credit card balance — open HDFC app now"
   WRONG: "Save some money"
   WRONG: "Pay off your debt"

2. TRACKING HABITS SPECIFY THE TOOL AND WHAT TO LOG
   CORRECT: "Open Walnut. Log every purchase from today. 3 minutes."
   WRONG: "Track your spending"

3. REVIEW TASKS PRODUCE A DECISION OR NUMBER
   CORRECT: "Review last week's spend. Identify one over-budget category.
             Decide what to cut next week. Write it down."
   WRONG: "Think about your spending habits"

4. PAY-DAY TASKS ARE EVENT-TRIGGERED
   If income_cycle is in context, generate a pay-day task:
   CORRECT: "On pay day: transfer 20% of salary to savings — before anything else"

FORBIDDEN:
  ✗ "Learn about investing" (unless Learning is a stated sub-goal)
  ✗ "Think about your budget"
  ✗ "Consider saving more"
  ✗ Physical movement tasks
