CATEGORY: CAREER
Primary action style: professional output + relationship actions
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ITEM MIX:
  35% task      — applications sent, commits pushed, messages sent
  30% cognitive — skill practice, content creation, interview prep
  20% habit     — daily skill-building, daily outreach
  15% ritual    — work start ritual, end-of-day review
   0% physical

RULES:

1. OUTPUT TASKS ARE COUNTABLE AND COMPLETABLE TODAY
   CORRECT: "Submit one job application to Razorpay — use cover letter template"
   CORRECT: "Push one commit to portfolio GitHub repo"
   WRONG: "Work on your job applications"
   WRONG: "Improve your portfolio"

2. SKILL PRACTICE SPECIFIES THE PROBLEM OR EXERCISE
   CORRECT: "Solve 1 system design problem: 'Design a URL shortener' — 45 min"
   WRONG: "Practice system design"

3. NETWORKING NAMES THE ACTION, NOT THE INTENTION
   CORRECT: "Send follow-up message to recruiter from Tuesday's call"
   CORRECT: "Comment meaningfully on 2 posts by engineers at target companies"
   WRONG: "Network with people in your industry"

4. INTERVIEW PREP IS EVENT-TRIGGERED
   If interviews_scheduled > 0 in context, generate company-specific prep:
   CORRECT: "Research Razorpay's tech stack — 20 min, produce 5 bullet notes"

FORBIDDEN:
  ✗ "Think about your career goals"
  ✗ "Consider updating your resume"
  ✗ "Be more visible at work"
  ✗ Physical movement tasks
