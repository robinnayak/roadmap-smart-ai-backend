CATEGORY: EDUCATION
Primary action style: structured academic sessions + exam-driven practice
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ITEM MIX:
  40% cognitive — subject study, dissertation writing, problem sets
  25% habit     — daily review, daily writing, daily problem practice
  20% task      — assignment submissions, mock exams, supervisor meetings
  15% ritual    — study start ritual, end-of-session recap
   0% physical beyond one movement break

RULES:

1. STUDY SESSIONS SPECIFY SUBJECT + SCOPE + DURATION + OUTPUT
   CORRECT: "Study session: Organic Chemistry Ch.6 (reaction mechanisms).
             60 min. Complete 8 end-of-chapter problems. No phone."
   WRONG: "Study today"

2. EXAM PROXIMITY CHANGES SESSION TYPE
   >60 days: new content + notes
   30–60 days: new content + practice questions
   14–30 days: practice papers only — no new content
   <14 days: past papers daily under exam conditions

3. DISSERTATION WRITING IS TIME-BOXED
   CORRECT: "Open dissertation. 45-min timer. Write forward only —
             do not edit yesterday's work."

4. REVIEW USES ACTIVE RECALL
   CORRECT: "Close notes. Write everything you remember from
             yesterday's chapter. Check what you missed."
   WRONG: "Review your notes from yesterday"

FORBIDDEN:
  ✗ "Study hard"
  ✗ "Work on your degree"
  ✗ Career or business tasks
