CATEGORY: FITNESS
Primary action style: physical movement + physical tracking
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ITEM MIX:
  40% physical  — the actual movement sessions
  25% habit     — hydration, sleep log, nutrition tracking
  20% ritual    — warm-up gates, cool-down, recovery
  15% task      — session logging, gear setup, physio booking
   0% cognitive — unless it is a specific study session with output

RULES:

1. WARM-UP IS ALWAYS A PREREQUISITE RITUAL
   Every training subgoal must have a warm-up ritual as its first
   item with is_prerequisite: true. Write it as a specific physical
   sequence — not "do a warm-up".
   CORRECT: "Do 10 leg swings each side, 15 bodyweight squats,
             30 sec high knees — then begin training block"
   WRONG: "Warm up before training"

2. EVERY WORKOUT NEEDS A MEASURABLE COMPLETION SIGNAL
   CORRECT: "Run 4×800m at target 5K pace with 200m walk recovery"
   CORRECT: "Complete 3 sets of 12 pull-ups with 90 sec rest"
   WRONG: "Go for a run"
   WRONG: "Do some strength work"

3. RECOVERY TASKS ARE PHYSICAL ACTIONS
   CORRECT: "Lie on back, legs up the wall, timer 10 minutes, no phone"
   CORRECT: "Foam roll quads and IT band — 60 sec each side"
   WRONG: "Rest and recover"
   WRONG: "Make sure to get enough sleep"

4. SESSION LOGGING IS MANDATORY
   Every training subgoal must end with a logging task.
   Specify what to record: distance, reps, pace, RPE, discomfort.

5. NUTRITION IS PHYSICAL TIMING
   CORRECT: "Drink recovery shake within 30 min — ingredients in fridge"
   WRONG: "Eat protein after your workout"

FORBIDDEN:
  ✗ "Research training plans" (unless producing a specific document)
  ✗ "Think about your form"
  ✗ "Consider adding more protein"
  ✗ Any task that uses item_type: cognitive for a movement session
  ✗ Typing a physical run or workout as 'project' or 'practice'
