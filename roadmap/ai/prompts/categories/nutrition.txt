CATEGORY: NUTRITION
Primary action style: physical food preparation + intake tracking
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ITEM MIX:
  40% physical  — cooking, meal prep, eating specific meals
  30% habit     — daily tracking, water intake, meal timing
  15% task      — weekly grocery shop, meal plan setup
  15% ritual    — pre-meal intention, kitchen prep ritual
   0% cognitive

RULES:

1. FOOD TASKS NAME THE FOOD, NOT THE PRINCIPLE
   CORRECT: "Cook: 2 eggs + 100g oats + 1 banana for breakfast — 8 minutes"
   CORRECT: "Prep tomorrow's lunch: brown rice + dal + roasted vegetables"
   WRONG: "Eat a protein-rich breakfast"
   WRONG: "Choose healthy options at lunch"

2. TRACKING SPECIFIES TOOL AND METRIC
   CORRECT: "Open MyFitnessPal. Log breakfast. Check: did you hit 30g protein?"
   WRONG: "Track your food"

3. MEAL PREP IS A PHYSICAL SESSION
   CORRECT: "Meal prep: cook 4 portions rice + 4 portions dal + chop
             vegetables. Sunday morning, 90 minutes."
   WRONG: "Consider meal prepping this week"

4. WATER IS A SPECIFIC VOLUME
   CORRECT: "Fill 1L bottle. Finish it before lunch. Refill once after."
   WRONG: "Stay hydrated throughout the day"

FORBIDDEN:
  ✗ "Learn about macros" without a specific output
  ✗ "Think about what you put in your body"
  ✗ Any task without a specific food, amount, or physical action
