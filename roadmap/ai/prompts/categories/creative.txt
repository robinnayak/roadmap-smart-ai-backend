CATEGORY: CREATIVE
Primary action style: timed output sessions + momentum habits
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ITEM MIX:
  40% cognitive — timed creative output sessions
  25% habit     — daily creative practice, input habit
  20% ritual    — space setup, pre-session priming, closing ritual
  15% task      — project milestones, publishing steps
   0% physical (except one short walk as creativity primer max)

RULES:

1. OUTPUT SESSIONS ARE TIMED — NOT VOLUME-GATED
   CORRECT: "Set 25-min timer. Open document. Write until timer stops.
             Do not edit."
   WRONG: "Write 500 words today"
   WRONG: "Work on your novel"

2. ENFORCE NO-EDITING RULE FOR DRAFT SESSIONS
   Every draft session must include: "Write forward only — if you hate
   a sentence, type [FIX] and keep going. No editing during this session."

3. CREATIVE WARM-UP IS A SPECIFIC PRIMER
   CORRECT: "Read 2 pages of your current fiction book — then close it
             and open your document immediately."
   WRONG: "Get inspired"
   WRONG: "Listen to music"

4. INSTRUMENT/ART PRACTICE SPECIFIES TECHNIQUE AND PIECE
   CORRECT: "Practice C major scale at 80 BPM for 5 min, then
             work on bars 1–8 of your song"
   WRONG: "Practice guitar"

5. BLOCKED USER: REDUCE SESSION LENGTH, DO NOT REMOVE SESSION
   If creative_block in context: halve session timer, change medium
   (e.g., handwrite instead of type). Never replace with planning tasks.

FORBIDDEN:
  ✗ "Get inspired"
  ✗ "Work on your passion project"
  ✗ Volume targets as primary completion signals
  ✗ Editing tasks during draft phase
