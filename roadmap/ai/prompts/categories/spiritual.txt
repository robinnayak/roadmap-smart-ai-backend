CATEGORY: SPIRITUAL
Primary action style: consistent contemplative practice + specific reflection
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ITEM MIX:
  40% ritual    — meditation, prayer, gratitude — the core practice
  35% habit     — daily reflection, reading, journaling
  15% cognitive — study of a text with a specific prompt
  10% task      — one-off setup tasks
   0% physical beyond a contemplative walk

RULES:

1. MEDITATION / PRAYER ITEMS GIVE THE EXACT TECHNIQUE
   CORRECT: "Sit upright, eyes closed. Focus on breath at nostrils.
             When mind wanders, return — no judgment. Timer: 10 min."
   WRONG: "Meditate today"

2. GRATITUDE IS SPECIFIC, NOT GENERIC
   CORRECT: "Write 3 specific things you are grateful for — name the
             person, moment, or detail. Not 'my health'. Yes: 'Riya
             texting to check on me yesterday.'"
   WRONG: "Write in your gratitude journal"

3. READING SPECIFIES THE TEXT AND SECTION
   CORRECT: "Read pages 40–52 of Bhagavad Gita commentary. Write one
             sentence: what does this mean for me today?"
   WRONG: "Read a spiritual text"

4. SESSION LENGTH MATCHES STREAK
   streak < 7 days:  max 5 min per session
   streak 7–21 days: up to 10 min per session
   streak > 21 days: up to 20 min per session
   Never generate 30-min sessions for a user who has never done 5 min.

FORBIDDEN:
  ✗ "Be more spiritual"
  ✗ "Find inner peace"
  ✗ Productivity, career, or finance tasks
  ✗ Any item that rushes or pressures the practice
