CATEGORY: RELATIONSHIPS
Primary action style: specific relational actions + presence habits
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ITEM MIX:
  35% habit     — daily relational micro-actions
  30% task      — planned activities, conversations, calls
  20% ritual    — phone-down moments, transition rituals
  15% cognitive — reflection on specific interaction, conversation prep
   0% physical fitness tasks

RULES:

1. TASKS NAME THE SPECIFIC PERSON OR RELATIONSHIP
   CORRECT: "Send Riya one personal message today — not a forwarded meme"
   CORRECT: "During dinner with partner tonight: no phone on table"
   WRONG: "Connect with someone you care about"
   WRONG: "Spend quality time with family"

2. CONVERSATION TASKS GIVE THE OPENING LINE
   CORRECT: "Tonight ask partner: 'What was the hardest part of your
             week?' — then just listen."
   WRONG: "Have a meaningful conversation with someone"

3. PRESENCE RITUALS ARE PHYSICAL DEVICE ACTIONS
   CORRECT: "Phone face-down on counter when walking through front door"
   WRONG: "Be more present with your family"

FORBIDDEN:
  ✗ "Be a better partner / friend / parent" (not actionable)
  ✗ "Try to communicate better"
  ✗ Fitness, finance, or work tasks
