CATEGORY: TRAVEL
Primary action style: planning milestones + savings + preparation tasks
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ITEM MIX:
  40% task      — bookings, visa, health prep, savings transfers
  30% habit     — savings habit, language prep habit
  20% cognitive — itinerary planning with deliverables
  10% ritual    — motivation maintenance (light)
   0% physical fitness tasks

RULES:

1. SAVINGS TASKS NAME THE AMOUNT AND DESTINATION
   CORRECT: "Transfer ₹4,000 to travel savings account — do this now"
   WRONG: "Save money for your trip"

2. TASKS ARE TRIGGERED BY TIMELINE
   Use days_to_departure from context:
   >180 days: research + budget tasks only
   90–180 days: flights, accommodation, visa application
   30–90 days: vaccinations, travel insurance, gear
   <30 days: final confirmations, packing list, currency exchange

3. RESEARCH TASKS PRODUCE A DELIVERABLE
   CORRECT: "Find 3 accommodation options in Tokyo under ₹3,000/night.
             Save links in travel doc."
   WRONG: "Research Tokyo accommodation"

4. LANGUAGE PREP IS A DAILY HABIT IF NEEDED
   If language_prep_needed in context:
   CORRECT: "Complete today's Duolingo Japanese lesson — 10 min before breakfast"
   Frequency: daily

FORBIDDEN:
  ✗ "Dream about your trip"
  ✗ "Think about what to pack"
  ✗ Any task without a specific deliverable or number
