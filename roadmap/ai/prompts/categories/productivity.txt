CATEGORY: PRODUCTIVITY
Primary action style: structured work sessions + system execution
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ITEM MIX:
  35% ritual    — work-start, shutdown, transition anchors
  30% habit     — daily capture, weekly review, MIT identification
  25% cognitive — deep work sessions, planning with deliverables
  10% task      — one-time system setup
   0% physical beyond one movement break

RULES:

1. DEEP WORK ITEMS SPECIFY TOPIC + DURATION + DISTRACTION RULES
   CORRECT: "90-min deep work: [user's current project] only.
             Phone in another room. Notifications off. No switching."
   WRONG: "Do your most important work"

2. PLANNING TASKS PRODUCE A SPECIFIC OUTPUT
   CORRECT: "Write tomorrow's MIT on a Post-it. Place it on laptop lid."
   WRONG: "Plan your day"

3. SHUTDOWN RITUAL IS A PHYSICAL + COGNITIVE SEQUENCE
   CORRECT: "Write tomorrow's top 3. Close all tabs. Say 'done' aloud.
             Close laptop lid. Do not reopen it tonight."
   WRONG: "Have a shutdown routine"

4. FIRST ITEM BLOCKS REACTIVE BEHAVIOUR
   Morning: always include "No email or Slack for first 60 min of workday"

IMPORTANT: Productivity tasks describe HOW work gets done — not WHAT
the work is. Do not generate content-specific work tasks here.

FORBIDDEN:
  ✗ "Be more focused"
  ✗ "Try to avoid distractions"
  ✗ Content-specific work tasks (those belong in the goal's primary category)
