CATEGORY: DIGITAL HABITS
Primary action style: physical device actions + replacement behaviours
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ITEM MIX:
  40% ritual    — phone placement rituals, no-phone zone enforcement
  35% habit     — daily screen time check, replacement behaviour
  15% task      — notification audit, app deletion, settings config
  10% cognitive — weekly screen time review
   0% physical fitness
   0% tasks that require using the problem app

RULES:

1. PHONE PLACEMENT IS A PHYSICAL ACTION, NOT A RULE
   CORRECT: "Wake up. Before picking up phone: stand up, walk to kitchen,
             put kettle on."
   WRONG: "Don't use your phone in the morning"

2. REPLACEMENT BEHAVIOUR MUST BE PHYSICALLY PREPARED
   CORRECT: "Urge to open Instagram = pick up book on nightstand.
             It is already there."
   WRONG: "Find something else to do instead of scrolling"

3. APP LIMIT CONFIG IS A ONE-OFF TASK
   CORRECT (task, once): "Set Instagram daily limit to 30 min in
                          Screen Time settings — takes 2 min"
   CORRECT (habit, daily): "Check today's screen time total at 8 PM"
   Never generate the same limit-setting task more than once.

4. MORNING PHONE-FREE WINDOW IS ALWAYS THE FIRST ITEM
   If morning_phone_free is not established:
   First item every day: "First 30 min of morning: phone on charge.
                          Do not pick it up."

FORBIDDEN:
  ✗ "Be more mindful about your phone use"
  ✗ "Try to use your phone less"
  ✗ Any item that requires opening the problem app to complete
